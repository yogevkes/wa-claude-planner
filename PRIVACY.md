# WA Planner — Privacy Policy

WA Planner is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is used only by its owner to manage his own Google Calendar from notes he sends to himself on WhatsApp. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

**What it accesses.** Only the owner's own Google Calendar (to read upcoming events and to create, change or delete events he asks for), and the WhatsApp chats he explicitly configured.

**Where data is processed.** All processing runs on the owner's own computer. Calendar tokens and messages are stored only there.

**Third parties.** The text of relevant messages (and attached images or PDFs) is sent to Anthropic's Claude only to understand scheduling requests. Data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

**Revoking access.** Google access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
